## Comprehensive Analysis Based on ALL Available Track Data

## METHODOLOGY

**Data Source**: Monthly revenue data from your music dashboard (August 2025 update)

**Tracks Analyzed**: 21 tracks with sufficient post-peak data (minimum 3 months after peak)
- Brazilian Funk: 2 tracks
- Phonk: 11 tracks  
- Electronic: 4 tracks

**Weighting Method**: Weighted by peak revenue (gives more influence to bigger, more successful tracks)
- This prevents small, unstable tracks from skewing the averages
- Reflects real-world portfolio impact more accurately

**Calculation**: Exponential decay formula
- Monthly decay rate: r = 1 - (remaining_ratio)^(1/months)
- 12-month projection: decline = 1 - (1 - r)^12
